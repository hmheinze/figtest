How the Scanner Works

Step 01 — Live Video Sampling

Continuous pixel extraction at 60fps

The scanner reads your front camera feed frame-by-frame using the browser's getUserMedia API. Each frame is drawn to a hidden canvas and pixel data is extracted via getImageData() — giving us raw RGBA values across your face in real time, no server required.

ctx.drawImage(video, 0, 0, 160, 120) → getImageData() → Uint8ClampedArray[R,G,B,A…]

Step 02 — Redness Detection

R/G channel ratio per zone

Redness is computed by dividing the mean Red channel value by the mean Green channel value within each facial zone. A healthy ratio sits near 1.15–1.25. Values above 1.4 indicate active redness or inflammation — such as rosacea, breakouts, or post-sun reaction. This is the same principle used in clinical dermatoscopy and L'Oréal's Skin Genius redness detection.

redness_score = mean(R[zone]) / mean(G[zone]) · <1.25 → No flag · 1.25–1.4 → Mild · >1.4 → Alert

Step 03 — Texture Analysis

Local luminance standard deviation

Texture is measured by computing the standard deviation of luminance values across a zone's pixels. Smooth, healthy skin has low variance — the pixel-to-pixel brightness difference is minimal. Congestion, enlarged pores, dry patches, and surface roughness all increase local variance, detected as higher texture score.

lum = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B texture_roughness = √( Σ(lum_i − mean)² / n )

Step 04 — Dark Circle Detection

Luminance delta between under-eye and cheek zones

Dark circles are identified by comparing the average luminance of the under-eye zone against the cheek zone. A larger negative delta (under-eye darker than cheeks) signals melanin deposition, vascular pooling from fatigue, or thinning skin. We weight this by your reported sleep hours for a calibrated score.

dark_circle_delta = mean_lum(cheek) − mean_lum(under_eye) · Δ <8 → Minimal · Δ 8–18 → Moderate · Δ >18 → Significant

Step 05 — Hydration Estimation

Brightness saturation proxy

Well-hydrated skin reflects light more evenly and has higher average pixel brightness. Dehydrated skin shows increased local variance and lower mean brightness due to fine surface texture from transepidermal water loss. This is combined with your reported water intake and environmental humidity for a contextual hydration score.

hydration_estimate = (mean_brightness × 0.5) + (evenness × 0.3) + (lifestyle_input × 0.2)

Step 06 — Contextual Calibration

Cycle phase + environment modifiers

Raw pixel scores are adjusted using hormonal phase modifiers (progesterone increases sebum → redness penalty in luteal phase), UV Index (photo-damage factor), AQI pollution load (oxidative stress penalty), sleep deficit (dark circle amplifier), and stress level (barrier disruption signal). The result is a contextualised skin intelligence score that reflects your skin as it is right now.
