Design Principles
Three principles guide every design decision in the ether.fi system.
✦
Clarity over complexity
DeFi is complex. Our UI isn't. Progressive disclosure, plain language, and obvious paths forward at every step.
⬡
Confidence through control
Non-custodial by architecture. Design communicates user ownership and control at every touchpoint.
◎
Earn, don't explain
Show yield, rewards, and real-world utility upfront. Justify complexity only after trust is established.
